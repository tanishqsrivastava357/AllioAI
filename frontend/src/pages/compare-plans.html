Plan questions

Still deciding?

Can I switch plans anytime?

Yes. You can move between available plans from your account, and your access updates according to the plan you choose.

What happens if I downgrade?

Your account keeps its data and conversations. Paid-only limits and features change when the new plan takes effect.

Do you offer refunds?

Refund eligibility depends on the purchase terms shown at checkout. Contact us if you need help with a billing question.

Which plan is right for a team?

Business is designed for shared workspaces, admin controls, and dedicated support. Talk to Sales if you need guidance.
