Overview

AllioAI is committed to protecting the privacy and security of the information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect personal data and other information when you access or use AllioAI’s website, applications, AI tools, workspaces, and related services.

By using our Services, you agree that your information may be processed in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable laws. We aim to keep this process transparent, secure, and minimal.

Information We Collect

We may collect information in the following ways:

  • Account information such as your name, email address, login details, and profile preferences.
  • Usage information including pages visited, feature usage, searches, session activity, and app interactions.
  • Content you provide, such as prompts, uploaded files, documents, images, project inputs, and chat history.
  • Device and technical information, including IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, and diagnostics.
  • Payment and billing information when you subscribe to a paid plan.
  • Customer support communications and feedback you submit to us.

We only collect information that is reasonably necessary to operate, secure, and improve the Services.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the AllioAI experience.
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts from unauthorized access.
  • Deliver AI features, including chat, content generation, research, analysis, and workspace tools.
  • Personalize settings, recommendations, and product experiences.
  • Respond to support requests and troubleshoot issues.
  • Monitor performance, detect abuse, and maintain platform security.
  • Fulfill legal obligations and enforce our terms and policies.

Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell personal data. We may share information only in the following limited situations:

  • With trusted service providers that support hosting, infrastructure, analytics, payments, or customer support.
  • When required by law, court order, regulation, or a valid legal request.
  • To protect the rights, safety, and security of AllioAI, our users, or the public.
  • During a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all of our assets, where applicable safeguards are in place.

Where possible, we use contractual protections and security requirements to limit third-party access to data.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including:

  • Accessing the personal data we hold about you.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details in the Contact section below.

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to provide the Services, fulfill legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our policies. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it in accordance with applicable law.
